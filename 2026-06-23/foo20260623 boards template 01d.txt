age: 40
sex: man
past medical history: negative except for taking ibuprofen PRN for back pain.
symptoms: presents to ED with 1 episode of melena last night without pain, nausea or vomiting.
physical exam: unremarkable, HR 75, BP 115/70
labs: hgb 13.5 (low), BUN 12 (nml), creatinine .7, eGFR >60
other findings: 

While in the ED VS remain stable, no further melena. Glasgow-Blachford bleeding score is 1

In addition to stopping ibuprofen, Which options are the best?:
A) Discharge home on oral PPI. plan for outpatient EGD
B) Repeat HGB in 12 hours and then decide about disposition
C) Maintain NPO in preparation for EGD in 24 hours
D) Start high-dose IV PPI and monitor Hgb level
E) null

my answer is C since he has had one likely episode of self-limited upper gi bleed from ibuprofen, but may rebleed at any time.
Your answer? Your reasons?
Correct is A since a GBS of 0 or 1 suggests very low risk of bleeding. No need for in patient EGD.
